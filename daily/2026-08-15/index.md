    question: "What black-white-and-gray painting technique did Picasso use for Guernica to mimic the immediacy of wartime newsprint photography?"
    answer: "Grisaille"
    topic: arts
    explanation: >-
      Picasso painted Guernica using grisaille, restricting himself to black,
      white, and shades of gray, and had a special matte paint mixed to keep
      the surface from catching gallery light with any gloss. The washed-out
      palette echoes the black-and-white newspaper photographs through which
      most people were experiencing the Spanish Civil War at the time,
      lending the huge mural the documentary urgency of a news bulletin
      rather than the richness of a traditional history painting.
  - id: "2026-08-15-2"
    question: "About how many weeks did Picasso take to paint the 25-foot-wide Guernica, working from May 1 to June 4, 1937?"
    answer: "Five"
    topic: arts
    explanation: >-
      Picasso had been commissioned to produce a large work for the Spanish
      Pavilion at the 1937 Paris World's Fair, but had no real subject until
      news broke of the German and Italian bombing of the Basque town of
      Gernika on April 26, 1937. Working at a furious pace in a Paris studio,
      he completed the roughly 11-by-25-foot canvas in about five weeks,
      finishing in time to be installed at the fair that summer.
  - id: "2026-08-15-3"
    question: "What photographer, Picasso's partner at the time, documented Guernica's creation in a sequence of 28 studio photographs?"
    answer: "Dora Maar"
    topic: arts
    explanation: >-
      Dora Maar, a photographer and Picasso's companion during this period,
      took a series of 28 photographs in his studio between May 11 and June
      4, 1937, capturing the painting at successive stages. Her photos are
      the main reason art historians can trace exactly how the composition
      evolved, including changes like the horse's ears shifting from
      pointing sideways to standing upright.
  - id: "2026-08-15-4"
    question: "Guernica spent over 40 years on loan at what New York museum, per Picasso's decree that it not go to Spain until democracy was restored there?"
    answer: "MoMA"
    topic: arts
    explanation: >-
      In 1939 Picasso placed Guernica on extended loan with New York's
      Museum of Modern Art, stipulating through his lawyer that it not be
      transferred to Spain until the country's democratic liberties were
      restored. That meant the painting stayed in exile at MoMA for over
      four decades, well past Picasso's death in 1973, only returning to
      Spanish soil in 1981 after Francisco Franco's dictatorship had ended.
  - id: "2026-08-15-5"
    question: "Whose February 2003 speech to the UN Security Council advocating for the Iraq War prompted staff to cover the Guernica tapestry outside with a blue curtain?"
    answer: "Colin Powell"
    topic: arts
    explanation: >-
      A woven tapestry reproduction of Guernica, commissioned by Nelson
      Rockefeller and made under Picasso's own supervision, had hung outside
      the UN Security Council chamber since 1985. When U.S. Secretary of
      State Colin Powell delivered his February 2003 case for military
      action against Iraq to reporters at that spot, the anti-war tapestry
      was hidden behind a blue curtain, a moment widely read as pointedly
      ironic given the artwork's origins.

  - id: "2026-07-31-1"
    question: "What is the largest planet in our solar system?"
    answer: "Jupiter"
    topic: science
    explanation: >-
      Jupiter is the largest planet in the solar system, with a mass more
      than twice that of all the other planets combined. Its Great Red Spot
      is a storm wider than Earth that has been raging for at least 350
      years, and it has 95 confirmed moons, including Ganymede — the
      largest moon in the solar system, bigger even than the planet Mercury.
  - id: "2026-07-31-2"
    question: "Which ancient wonder of the world was located in Giza, Egypt?"
    answer: "The Great Pyramid of Giza"
    topic: history
    explanation: >-
      Built around 2560 BCE as a tomb for the pharaoh Khufu, the Great
      Pyramid of Giza is the oldest of the Seven Wonders of the Ancient
      World and the only one still largely intact. It was the tallest
      man-made structure on Earth for over 3,800 years, a record unmatched
      until England's Lincoln Cathedral was completed in the 14th century.
  - id: "2026-07-31-3"
    question: "What is the longest river in the world?"
    answer: "The Nile River"
    topic: geography
    explanation: >-
      The Nile stretches roughly 6,650 kilometers (4,130 miles) through
      northeastern Africa, flowing north into the Mediterranean Sea. It has
      two major tributaries — the White Nile and Blue Nile — and its annual
      floods deposited the fertile silt that sustained ancient Egyptian
      civilization for thousands of years.
  - id: "2026-07-31-4"
    question: "Who developed the theory of general relativity?"
    answer: "Albert Einstein"
    topic: biography
    explanation: >-
      Einstein published his theory of general relativity in 1915,
      describing gravity not as a force but as the curvature of spacetime
      caused by mass and energy. It was confirmed in 1919 when astronomers
      observed starlight bending around the Sun during a solar eclipse,
      making Einstein an overnight scientific celebrity.
  - id: "2026-07-31-5"
    question: "What gas do plants absorb from the atmosphere during photosynthesis?"
    answer: "Carbon dioxide"
    topic: science
    explanation: >-
      Plants use light energy to convert carbon dioxide and water into
      glucose and oxygen, a process called photosynthesis that occurs
      mainly in chloroplasts. The oxygen released as a byproduct is what
      most of Earth's life, including humans, depends on to breathe.

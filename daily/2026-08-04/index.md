    question: "About how many years did Michelangelo spend painting the Sistine Chapel ceiling?"
    answer: "Four"
    topic: arts
    explanation: >-
      Michelangelo worked on the ceiling from 1508 to 1512, roughly four
      years, a grueling stretch during which he designed his own scaffold —
      a flat wooden platform built out from brackets set into holes near the
      top of the chapel's windows, rather than a structure built up from the
      floor. Partway through, in 1510, he took a yearlong break; the frescoes
      painted after that break are noticeably bolder and more confident than
      the ones that came before it.
  - id: "2026-08-04-2"
    question: "Contrary to popular belief, Michelangelo painted the Sistine Chapel ceiling in what position rather than lying on his back?"
    answer: "Standing"
    topic: arts
    explanation: >-
      The enduring image of Michelangelo flat on his back is a myth — he
      actually stood upright on his scaffold, craning his neck backward and
      reaching his brush above his head for hours at a time. The physical
      toll was still severe: he wrote of the strain leaving his beard
      pointed at the ceiling and paint dripping into his eyes, and he later
      described his body as twisted out of shape by the years of awkward
      posture.
  - id: "2026-08-04-3"
    question: "What art form did Michelangelo consider his true calling, having resented being pulled off a tomb commission to paint the ceiling instead?"
    answer: "Sculpture"
    topic: arts
    explanation: >-
      Michelangelo had come to Rome to build an elaborate marble tomb for
      Pope Julius II and considered himself a sculptor first, not a painter —
      he had barely worked in fresco before. When Julius redirected him to
      the ceiling instead, Michelangelo reportedly suspected a rival had
      pushed the commission on him hoping he'd fail at an unfamiliar medium,
      and he only returned to the tomb project, unfinished, decades later.
  - id: "2026-08-04-4"
    question: "How many scenes from the Book of Genesis run along the center of the Sistine Chapel ceiling?"
    answer: "Nine"
    topic: arts
    explanation: >-
      Nine Genesis panels run down the spine of the ceiling, from the
      Separation of Light and Darkness through the Drunkenness of Noah,
      including the famous Creation of Adam. They alternate between large
      and small scenes and are framed by painted architecture and dozens of
      other figures, all part of a scheme far more elaborate than what
      Michelangelo was originally commissioned to paint.
  - id: "2026-08-04-5"
    question: "About how many individual figures did Michelangelo ultimately paint across the ceiling, far beyond the dozen apostles he was originally asked for?"
    answer: "300"
    topic: arts
    explanation: >-
      Julius II's original brief called for just the twelve apostles in
      simple decorative roundels. Michelangelo talked him into a far more
      ambitious scheme instead, and by the time the ceiling was finished it
      held roughly 300 figures — prophets, sibyls, nude youths, and the
      Genesis narrative — making it one of the most densely populated works
      in the history of painting.
